From upload to explanation

Every X-ray goes through the same four steps, and each one is visible in your results.

  1. 1

    Enhance

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Classify

    Abnormal scans go on to separate pneumonia, tuberculosis and COVID-19.

  4. 4

    Explain

    Heatmaps show where each stage looked when it made its call.

What it looks for

Four possible outcomes, each with its own typical pattern on a radiograph.

  • Normal

    Clear, dark lung fields with sharp heart and diaphragm borders.

  • Pneumonia

    Patchy or lobar white areas where air spaces fill with fluid.

  • Tuberculosis

    Often upper-zone infiltrates, nodules or cavities.

  • COVID-19

    Frequently hazy, ground-glass shadows on both sides, toward the edges.
