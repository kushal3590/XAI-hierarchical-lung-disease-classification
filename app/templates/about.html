Built to make chest X-ray AI easier to question

PulmoLens is the demonstration app for An XAI-Driven Multiscale Framework for Hierarchical Lung Disease Classification, a final-year project in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BMS College of Engineering.

The idea

Most chest X-ray classifiers give a label and stop there. This project pairs a two-stage classifier with several explanation methods, so anyone reviewing a result can see what it was based on and spot when the model has learned the wrong thing.

That focus shaped the model itself: the team used the explanations to find shortcut patterns, then retrained with those regions masked out.

Under the hood

Preprocessing
CLAHE contrast enhancement, 224 × 224 resize
Explanations
Grad-CAM, SHAP and LIME
Training data
Five public chest X-ray datasets
App
Flask and PyTorch, running locally
